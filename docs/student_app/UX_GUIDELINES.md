# UX & Accessibility Guidelines

## 1. Design Aesthetics & Visual Tokens
- Color Palette: Deep Slate `#0F172A`, Dark Card `#1E293B`, Accent Teal `#0D9488`, Accent Cyan `#06B6D4`, Accent Orange `#F97316`.
- Typography: Google Fonts Inter with high contrast hierarchy.
- Accessibility: High contrast ratios, screen reader semantics, and color-blind friendly diagnostic badges.
